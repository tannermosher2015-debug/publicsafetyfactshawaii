## Accuracy notes

Checked line by line against the article. What changed from the first draft, and why:

1. **"The Council decides August 24" → "the committee takes it up again."** August 24 is the Housing and Land Use Committee, not the full Council. The committee can recommend passage or file the bills; a recommendation still goes to the full Council for readings. The committee has also already recessed twice without voting (July 29 and August 10), so a vote on the 24th is possible, not certain. "One vote" was dropped for the same reason.
2. **"the Chief told the County Council" → attribution moved on screen** as "to a Council committee." Same correction, and it frees the VO line.
3. **Hook rewritten for pacing.** The original hook ran 21 words in 4 seconds — roughly 315 wpm, about double a normal narration rate. Attribution moved to a lower third; the spoken line is now 11 words in 5 seconds. All four beats were rebalanced to land at 30 seconds even.
4. **Navy corrected to `#0F1F3D`.** The draft's `#0D1F3C` is close but not the site's `--smoke`. Gold `#C9A84C` was already exact (`--ember`).
5. **Added the records appeal** to the "real fight" beat. The article names three open questions — title, the pending OIP appeal, and burials — and the draft carried only two.
6. **Added "oral testimony is closed"** to the CTA and caption. A "one vote, link in bio" CTA reads as an invitation to show up and testify. Oral testimony closed July 29; written is still accepted through mauicounty.us. Saying so prevents sending people to a mic that is turned off.

Verified as accurate and unchanged:

- The URL. The file is `haiku-fire-station-coverage-gap.md` but its frontmatter slug is `haiku_fire_station_east_maui_coverage_gap`, and `/posts/$slug` routes on the slug. The link in the draft resolves.
- **Two hours** — Chief Ventura, quoted in the article as "two hours away from the Pāʻia community."
- **Eighteen years** — parcel bought December 2007.
- **Zero stations in Haʻikū** — county lists ten Maui stations, none in Haʻikū; nearest are Pāʻia at six miles and Makawao at eight.
- **"Not IF. WHERE."** — matches the article's "a room divided over where one can lawfully and honorably be built."
- Neutral framing throughout, per the article's own disclaimer.

Do not say, on this piece:

- That the county has clear title, or that it does not. Both are asserted in the record and neither is settled.
- That the Council is "taking" or "dispossessing" land, or conversely that the claim is meritless.
- That the OIP appeal has been decided. It has not.
- That burials were found in the station footprint. Eleven test trenches found no subsurface cultural features there; the reported burials are up the ridge, outside it, and the waterline route had only a pedestrian survey.
